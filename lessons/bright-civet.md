Before treating target access or redistribution evidence as new owner work, recover the repository's retained artifacts and qualification receipts, then compare exact dependency and native binary identities. A current public asset listing cannot establish what was built, handed out or qualified previously.

As of 2026-11-02, the primary raised a Microsoft runtime redistribution-basis question before recovering the earlier Windows ZIPs and target trial. The operator corrected that Windows CI and a local Parallels environment were already available. The retained 0.2.1 and 0.2.2 bundles contain the same pikepdf runtime, pi-heif native libraries and MuPDF bytes as the current 0.2.4 build. The earlier receipts preserve organisation-only use and do not claim complete native-source closure or public binary distribution. Recovery therefore removes the invented new-target/dependency premise without manufacturing a licensing grant or erasing the still-needed public redistribution qualification.

The correction preserves the historical ZIPs, compares exact bytes, continues the existing Windows CI route and distinguishes ARM64 emulation from hosted x64 qualification. A local Parallels wrapper failed before executing the requested VM command when its internal process observation was refused; that failure neither proves the VM absent nor authorizes initialization, reset or an alternate bypass. Reading the wrapper afterward corrected an earlier overstatement: it automatically attempted initialization after the failed process check, and those initialization attempts also failed. No VM command ran; do not claim nothing beyond a wrapper check executed without reading its control flow. This provisional lesson records evidence recovery, not a new rule or permission.

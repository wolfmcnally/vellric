Use structured sequential subprocess results, or separate tool calls whose outcomes are read immediately; a later successful formatter is not evidence that an earlier test passed. Discover and regenerate every independent fixture/inventory of a changed contract before the full gate. Do not weaken its assertion or change a budget to hide drift.

The final restored focused suite passed. This is an unsettled process lesson, not a graduated rule or authority to change the shared toolchain.
